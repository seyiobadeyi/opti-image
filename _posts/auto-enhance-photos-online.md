## What Auto-Enhance Actually Does

Auto-enhance is not a filter. It is a set of automatic image adjustments applied based on an analysis of your specific photo. When you click "Enhance," Optimage evaluates the tonal range, colour channels, and exposure of your image and applies targeted corrections:

- **Brightness** — lifts underexposed shadows and pulls back blown highlights so detail is visible across the whole image
- **Contrast** — stretches the tonal range so blacks look black and whites look white, not grey
- **Saturation** — boosts muted colours to natural vibrancy without oversaturating skin tones
- **White balance** — corrects colour casts from artificial lighting (orange indoor light, blue shade) so whites appear neutral

The result is a photo that looks like it was taken under better conditions or by a more experienced photographer — without you touching a single slider.

## Does Auto-Enhance Work on Dark Photos

Yes. Underexposed and dark photos are where auto-enhance produces the most dramatic results. The algorithm lifts shadows while protecting highlights from blowing out, which is the same goal as manually adjusting exposure and shadow sliders in Lightroom. Very severely underexposed photos (completely black with almost no detail) will see limited improvement because there is no recoverable information in the dark pixels — but any photo with visible subject detail can be meaningfully improved.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What does auto-enhance do to a photo?**
Auto-enhance analyses the image and automatically adjusts brightness, contrast, saturation, and white balance. It is designed to fix common problems — underexposure, low contrast, colour casts from artificial lighting — without requiring any manual slider adjustments.

**Is auto-enhance the same as editing?**
Auto-enhance is a form of editing — it applies real tonal and colour corrections to your image. The difference from manual editing is that the adjustments are calculated automatically based on the photo's own characteristics rather than set by a human operator. The results are strong for typical photos but do not match the precision of a skilled manual edit in Lightroom or Photoshop.

**Does auto-enhance work on dark photos?**
Yes, and dark or underexposed photos typically show the most improvement. The tool lifts shadows and boosts exposure while protecting highlights. Very severely underexposed images (almost fully black) have limited recoverable detail, but any photo with a visible subject can be noticeably improved.

**What is the best free alternative to Lightroom for auto-correction?**
Optimage's [auto-enhance tool](/enhance) is the fastest free alternative for JPEG, PNG, and WebP images. It performs the same category of corrections as Lightroom's "Auto" button — in a browser, with no subscription or installation required.
