## What Photographers Actually Need From a Gallery Platform

Client gallery platforms exist to solve a specific problem: how do you get high-resolution images from your device to your client's screen in a way that looks professional, protects your work, and gives clients a clear way to select and download their photos?

The core features that matter are:

1. **A clean, mobile-friendly gallery view** — clients need to browse easily on a phone
2. **PIN or password protection** — galleries should not be publicly accessible
3. **Download controls** — choose whether clients can download web-resolution previews, full resolution, or nothing
4. **Favourites / selection** — clients mark the images they want, reducing back-and-forth
5. **No client account required** — asking clients to create accounts creates drop-off

Pixieset does all of this well. The question is whether you need to pay for it.

## Pixieset's Pricing Reality

Pixieset has a free plan, but it is limited. The free tier gives you 3 GB of storage — enough for a handful of galleries — and does not include custom branding, store features, or the full set of presentation options. To get a usable gallery experience for regular client delivery, most photographers end up on the Basic plan at around $8/month, or the Plus plan at around $16/month for more storage and features.

Over a year, that is $96–$192 for gallery software alone — before you factor in editing software, website hosting, and other business costs. For photographers just starting out, that recurring cost adds up fast.

## What Optimage Offers Free

Optimage's [client galleries](/galleries) feature gives you:

Watermarking proofs before client selection, then delivering clean full-res files on approval, is a workflow Optimage handles end-to-end for free.

## When Pixieset (or SmugMug) Is Worth the Cost

Optimage is not the right answer for every photographer. Pixieset and SmugMug offer things Optimage does not:

- **Print sales and store integration** — if you sell prints directly from your gallery, Pixieset's store is a significant feature.
- **Mobile apps** — Pixieset has iOS and Android apps for both you and your clients.
- **Bookings and contracts** — the higher Pixieset tiers integrate with scheduling and client management tools.
- **Long-term portfolio hosting** — SmugMug in particular is built for permanent portfolio display and print sales.

If you are an established photographer monetising print sales or managing client relationships through an integrated platform, the monthly fee for Pixieset or SmugMug is likely worth it. If you are primarily delivering digital files to clients — wedding photos, portrait sessions, headshots, event photography — and you do not need store or booking features, Optimage's free galleries do the job without the subscription.

## A Typical Workflow With Optimage

1. Import your edited JPEGs or PNGs into Optimage.
2. Apply [watermarks](/watermark) to low-resolution proofs for the selection round.
3. Upload the proofed images to a new gallery, set a PIN, and set downloads to "view only."
4. Share the gallery link and PIN with your client. They browse on any device, no account needed, and heart their favourites.
5. After selection, [compress](/compress) and [resize](/resize) the chosen images to delivery resolution, remove the watermarks, and create a new gallery with full-resolution download enabled.
6. Send the final gallery link.

The entire process — compression, watermarking, gallery creation, client delivery — stays in one free tool.

For photographers who want professional client delivery without a monthly subscription, Optimage is a genuine Pixieset alternative for the core gallery workflow. You gain the same PIN-protected, client-friendly gallery experience alongside a full image processing suite, at no cost.
